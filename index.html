Who I Am

Curious researcher. Dedicated leader. Aspiring engineer.

Hello! I am Kayra Ecrin Küçük, an IB Diploma candidate at Bilkent Erzurum Laboratory School (BELS) in Erzurum, Türkiye. Driven by a deep curiosity for physical laws and mechanical systems, my academic ambition is to pursue an undergraduate degree in Mechanical Engineering.

My academic profile combines a rigorous STEM foundation — achieving Triple 7s in Higher Level (HL) Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry with an official predicted score of 43 / 45 — with authentic engineering research. As a high school intern at Ataturk University’s Mechanical Engineering Department under Prof. Dr. Isak Kotcioglu, I investigated "Magnetic Braking Systems as a Future Innovation in Thermodynamics". In addition, I completed the Oxford Royale Summer Schools Engineering programme (covering Civil, Mechanical, and Electrical disciplines), earning an Achievement Award with an overall Grade A (86%).

Beyond the laboratory, my experiences encompass active school leadership, the performing arts, and competitive athletics. I founded and led our school's MUN Community for 4 years (mentoring 15 delegates, passing 3 GA resolutions, and earning recognition at MUN Bilbao, Spain), established LeoPard's Photography Studio (@leop.ardsstudio) to connect amateur photographers internationally, performed the lead role of Kreon in Antigone before 1,000 spectators, and hold a 4th Degree Blue Belt in Boxing alongside team leadership as Girls' Football Team Captain.
