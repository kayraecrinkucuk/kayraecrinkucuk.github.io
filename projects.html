Featured · University Engineering Research Ataturk University

Magnetic Braking Systems as a Future Innovation in Thermodynamics Dept of Mechanical Engineering

High school research internship conducted under the mentorship of Prof. Dr. Isak Kotcioglu at Ataturk University. Investigated non-contact electromagnetic deceleration via eddy current induction, evaluating the conversion of kinetic energy into thermal dissipation, surface heat distribution, and wear-free braking efficiency for high-speed transit and machinery.
